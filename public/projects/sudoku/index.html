Grandma's Sudoku Robot

A one-button puzzle machine, built for my grandma

My grandma does a sudoku every day. Her favorite puzzle website slowly filled up with ads — popups over the grid, fake download buttons, the works. Watching her fight through that just to get a puzzle felt wrong.

So I built her a robot. It's a single icon in the middle of her desktop. She double-clicks it, picks a difficulty, and a fresh puzzle comes out of her printer — with a quote at the top and a note from the family at the bottom. No browser, no ads, no accounts. Two clicks, one puzzle.

How the real one works

Python and tkinter for the little window, a backtracking generator for the puzzles, ReportLab to lay out the PDF, and the system print queue to send it straight to her printer. PyInstaller wraps the whole thing into one .exe so nothing ever needs installing. Source on GitHub →
