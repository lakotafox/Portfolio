Academic achievements

Question 2

Extracurricular activities

“In paragraph form, describe extracurricular activities — community involvement, talents, leadership, hobbies.”

Most of my free time goes to volunteering with Portland's parks and trees. I'm on the Tuesday morning stewardship crew at Hoyt Arboretum, pulling invasive plants, mulching, and planting natives, and I volunteer with the Bird Alliance of Oregon. This fall I'm training with Friends of Trees as a volunteer crew leader so I can help run public tree-planting events.

I also like using what I'm learning to help the people around me. Using the city's open data, I mapped every redwood in Portland plus 19,000 other heritage trees (lakotafox.com/redwoods) and every public fruit and nut tree in the city so neighbors can forage respectfully (lakotafox.com/olliesfruitmap). I've also built websites for a few small local businesses and wrote a program that prints personalized sudoku puzzles for my grandma. When I'm not doing any of that, I make art with code.

Question 3

Why this financial aid is necessary

“Explain circumstances that would make this financial aid necessary to further your education.”

I pay my own way through dog walking and gig work, and most of what I make goes to rent and food. The hard part is timing: SLCC is discontinuing my degree program, and enrolled students have until Summer 2027 to finish — after that the degree can't be completed at all. My tuition this fall is $2,324, due in September, and there isn't much room in my budget for a bill that size. This scholarship would go straight to tuition and let me finish before the door closes.

Question 5

My goals — and why me

“Your specific goals for post high school education, and why you should be the recipient of this scholarship.”

I want to finish my Computer Science & Information Systems degree at Salt Lake Community College before the program ends in 2027, then keep growing my freelance work building technology that treats people right. I've already started: I helped a salon leave a subscription platform for a system they own outright, and I build AI tools that run in the browser instead of a datacenter. Next I want to help older people avoid getting scammed and help everyday people keep control of their own data. This scholarship gets me to the finish line.
