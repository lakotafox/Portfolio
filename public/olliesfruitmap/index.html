    ℹ️ about this map — data & fruit-picking manners

    Tree locations come from three City of Portland Urban Forestry open datasets: the Heritage Trees registry, the Street Tree Inventory, and the Parks Tree Inventory. Apples and figs load first; the other seven groups load when you tap them on. Ornamental look-alikes that don't fruit (callery pear, flowering cherries, crabapples) are filtered out — but "flowering plum" stays, because its little cherry plums are real and delicious.

    Manners: street trees belong to everyone, yard trees belong to somebody — knock and ask, most people are thrilled to share. Never eat anything you can't identify yourself. Take a little, leave a lot.

    Caveat: much of the street inventory was collected by volunteers between 2010 and 2019; trees grow and some are gone. Ages marked "est." are estimated from trunk size. A tree marked "old orchard survivor" likely predates the neighborhood around it.
