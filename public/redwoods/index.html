    The Laurelhurst walk

    Laurelhurst Park grows all three surviving members of the redwood subfamily along one stroll — giant sequoias, coast redwoods, and deciduous dawn redwoods, a species known only from fossils until living trees were found in China in the 1940s. Start near SE Oak & César E. Chávez and wander east.

    About this map — data sources & caveats

    Tree locations come from three City of Portland Urban Forestry open datasets: the Heritage Trees registry, the Street Tree Inventory, and the Parks Tree Inventory, filtered to the three living species of the redwood subfamily and merged into a single snapshot.

    Caveat: much of the street inventory was collected by volunteers between 2010 and 2019. Trees grow, and some are gone — treat the map as a very good guide, not gospel.

    Etiquette: many heritage trees stand on private property. Admire them from the sidewalk.
